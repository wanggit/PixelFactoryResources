Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Pixel Depot ("the App", "we", "us") is a single-player puzzle game published by the developer listed on its App Store page. This policy explains exactly what information the App handles. It is written to be read in one minute.

We collect nothing. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter and no third-party tracking of any kind in the current build. The App sends us no information about you — the only network requests it makes fetch new puzzle levels from our own static hosting, and those requests carry nothing that identifies you (see "Level content updates" below).

Information stored on your device

The App keeps a small amount of state so your progress survives between sessions. It lives only in the operating system's private per-app storage on your own device:

  • Level progress and star ratings
  • Coin balance (the in-game soft currency)
  • Audio preferences (sound effects on/off, music on/off)

This data never leaves your device. We cannot read it, back it up, or recover it. Uninstalling the App deletes it permanently.

Level content updates

The App can download additional puzzle levels from our own static hosting, so new content can reach you without waiting for an App Store update. These requests:

  • carry no personal information — no account, no device identifier, no advertising identifier, no analytics payload, no query parameters; just a plain fetch of a public file
  • happen in the background and never block you from playing
  • are optional in every sense: with no network connection at all, the App is fully playable using the levels already stored on your device

Downloaded levels are kept in the operating system's private per-app storage alongside your progress, and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard web server access logs, including IP addresses, in the same way that any website does for any visitor. We do not receive, read, analyse or use those logs, and we have no way to link them to you.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see, receive or store your payment card, Apple ID or billing details. As part of their standard developer reporting, Apple and Google may show us aggregated, non-identifying records such as "a purchase of item X occurred in country Y"; these contain no personal information.

Advertising and tracking

The current build contains no advertising SDK and no tracking. We do not request the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and therefore do not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt. If a future version adds advertising or analytics, this policy and the App Store privacy declarations will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be listed here with a new "Last updated" date.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. It contains no social features, no chat and no user-generated content.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. Any right you have over the data above is exercised directly on your device (for example by deleting the App).

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the Support page for the current contact address.
