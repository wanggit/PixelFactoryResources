One tap. A whole factory answers.

Every level starts as a complete pixel-art picture. Tap a coloured crate and a stream of little robots marches onto the board, picks up blocks of its own colour and carries them back to the depot. Clear the whole board and the artwork joins your collection.

The only decision is order.

A crate can only send robots out while blocks of its colour are still reachable — bury a colour under the rest of the picture and the crate holding it sits in its slot with nothing to do. Fill every slot with crates that cannot work and the factory jams. Read the picture, count what is left, choose your crates in the right order.

What is in the box

281 levels, 35 chapters

A chapter map that always shows how far you are and what comes next. New levels can arrive without an App Store update.

An album that keeps growing

Fill a themed page of artworks and claim a reward — every fifth page a permanent upgrade.

Daily artwork

One level a day pays triple coins and fills its own shelf in the album. Missing a day costs nothing but that day's coins.

Seven-day rewards

A login ladder that climbs day by day, then starts over. Missing a day resets nothing.

Rope-linked crates

A tied pair ships together, takes two slots, and leaves only when both are empty.

Coins with a purpose

Permanent Depot upgrades and robot skins to spend them on, so every level you clear sticks with you.

Fair by design

Every shipped level is proven solvable by an exhaustive solver, then screened by a blind-play jam probe. A jam always means the order was wrong, never that the board was impossible. Two optional boosters — Extra Slot and Reveal — are bought with coins before a shift starts and never edit the level. The first three levels teach you with a pointing hand and a pulsing ring, without a word of text.

Private and offline

Pixel Depot collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no advertising SDK. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected across every category, and the whole game plays offline. Free to download and free to play all the way through; optional coin packs are never required to finish any level.
