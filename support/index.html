How to play

  1. Tap a coloured box at the bottom to slide it onto the slot bar.
  2. Robots stream out of each working box, pick up blocks of its colour and carry them to the depot.
  3. When a box runs out, its slot frees up for the next one.
  4. Clear every block to finish the level and add the artwork to your collection.

The one thing to watch: a box can only send robots out while blocks of its colour are still reachable. A colour buried under the rest of the picture leaves its box sitting idle in the slot. Fill every slot with boxes that cannot work, with no tappable box left to dig you out, and the factory jams.

Rope-linked crates

Some crates are tied to a partner in another column with a rope.

  • The rope looks tight and bright when both crates are at the head of their column — that pair is tappable. Tapping either one ships both, into two slots at once.
  • The rope looks slack and dim when the partner has not come up yet. Tapping now does nothing but a shake; nothing is broken.
  • A tied crate that runs empty stays in its slot, drawn hollow with a knot and no number, until its partner empties too. They leave together.

Frequently asked

My progress disappeared.

Progress, stars, coins and audio preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting or reinstalling the App removes them; we cannot restore them because we never receive them.

How do I turn the sound or music off?

Home screen → gear icon (top right) → Sound Effects / Music.

I'm stuck on a level.

The ↻ button in the top bar restarts the current level. Nothing is lost by restarting except the run in progress. You keep the stars and artwork from any level you have already cleared. Your first restart of an attempt re-deals the crate queue, so the same jam does not simply replay. Every restart after that replays the queue exactly as it was — the board is deterministic, and knowing that is what makes planning worth it. Clearing the level starts a fresh attempt, with a fresh free re-deal.

The factory jams even though blocks are left on the board.

That is the puzzle, not a bug. The remaining blocks of the colours in your slots are buried, so no robot can reach them, and no box you can tap would free a slot. Restart and open your boxes in a different order. When a jam leaves crates still waiting in the queue, the jam screen also offers Continue: for 150 coins the depot hauls away everything one clogged crate still owed, freeing its slot while the rest of the board stays exactly as you left it. Rope-linked crates cannot be continued (a rope is a two-crate commitment), and a jam with an empty queue cannot be bought out of — restarting is the way out of that one.

What are the boosters on the panel before a level starts?

Two, both paid in coins, both lasting only that one shift:

  • Extra Slot (90 coins) adds one more parking cell to the slot bar. It is worth the most on a level with rope-linked crates, because a tied pair needs two free cells at the same moment.
  • Reveal (60 coins) shows the real colour and count of every crate below the tappable row, instead of question marks.

Neither one edits the level. The same board is still there, and every level is certified solvable with no boosters at all — they buy you room and information, never a pass. Start Shift always works without buying anything.

I bought a booster and then the factory jammed. Do I have to buy it again?

Yes. A booster is spent on the shift you just played, so the panel asks again when you restart. This is deliberate: a booster that survived every retry would turn a hard level into a paid level, and the players who never buy one would be left with the harder version of the same game.

Why does the booster panel not show up on some levels?

Because no booster would do anything there. The first levels already show every crate, so Reveal has nothing left to reveal and is not offered at all — rather than let you pay for something that cannot help. On those same levels Extra Slot is greyed out with a note, since it works but the teaching levels never jam.

Where is the store, and what can I buy?

Home screen → gear icon (top right) → Store, or the coin pill for coin upgrades. The store sells coin packs, the Piggy Bank and a one-time Starter Pack. Coins are then spent inside the game on level boosters, permanent Depot Throughput upgrades and robot skins.

Is anything required to finish the game?

No. Every level is solvable with no purchases at all, there are no ads and no timers, and boosters never edit a level — they only give you one more parking cell or show you the crates that were hidden. Purchases skip waiting, never puzzle-solving.

What is the Piggy Bank?

Every level you clear for the first time puts 10 extra coins into a piggy bank — on top of the coins that go into your wallet, never taken out of it. When it is full you can break it open and collect everything inside. There are three tiers (500, 1,200 and 3,000 coins) and each one costs more than the last, so the first tier is the best value per coin in the whole game. The home screen shows how much you have saved so far.

I paid but my coins did not arrive.

Restart the App first: an unfinished transaction is delivered automatically on the next launch, which covers most cases (a purchase interrupted by a phone call, a low battery, or closing the App too early). If it is still missing, open Store → Restore purchases. If that does not help either, contact us with the receipt from your App Store or Google Play email and we will sort it out — we would rather lose the sale than keep your money.

How do I restore purchases on a new device?

Store → Restore purchases. This restores the Starter Pack and the Piggy Bank tiers you have already broken. Coin packs are consumable, so a pack you already spent cannot be restored — but a pack that was paid for and never delivered will be delivered again on the next launch.

Why does the store say "Store unavailable"?

The App could not reach the store, or the products are not on sale for your region yet. Nothing has been charged. Check your connection and tap Retry.

What currency am I charged in?

Prices shown in the store come from Apple or Google, in your own currency and including any local taxes. We never set or see the final amount.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) — we do not process payments and cannot issue refunds ourselves. Use reportaproblem.apple.com, or the order history in the Play Store app.

What is the daily artwork?

One level a day is marked as today's artwork on the home screen. Clearing it pays triple coins, once per day, and it fills a slot on the Daily Artworks page in your album. Replaying it on the same day does not pay again; a new day picks a new level, always from a different theme than yesterday. Missing a day costs nothing but that day's coins.

I missed a day of the seven-day rewards.

Nothing resets. The seven-day rewards count how many days you have claimed, not how many in a row, so you simply pick up on the next day's amount. After day seven the ladder starts over at day one, so there is always a next reward.

What do I get for filling an album page?

Coins, and every fifth page a permanent Depot Throughput level — the same upgrade the coin shop sells, so it stacks with the levels you buy. Once upgrades are maxed out, those milestone pages pay double coins instead.

The game is too fast / too slow.

There is no speed button. Once every box has been sent to the slot bar there are no decisions left to make, so the game speeds itself up to 2x for the run-out. Before that point it stays at 1x so you can read the board.

Why is a crate hollow with no number?

It is a rope-linked crate that has run empty and is waiting for its partner to empty too. They free their two slots at the same moment.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game is fully offline.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising SDK in the current build. See the Privacy Policy.
